Congratulations to Arjun Badola and Dikshit Hegde on winning the Qualcomm Innovation Fellowship India 2026. Their proposal, ‘Frequency-Adaptive Prior Supervision for Transparent 3D Gaussian Splatting’, is advised by Prof. Shanmuganathan Raman at IIT Gandhinagar.
