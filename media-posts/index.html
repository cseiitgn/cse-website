Arjun Badola and Dikshit Hegde win QIF India 2026

Congratulations to Arjun Badola and Dikshit Hegde on winning the Qualcomm Innovation Fellowship India 2026. Their proposal, ‘Frequency-Adaptive Prior Supervision for Transparent 3D Gaussian Splatting’, is advised by Prof. Shanmuganathan Raman at IIT Gandhinagar.

Caption text
Illustration note

The torus is a doughnut-shaped surface. The Gaussian version uses overlapping elliptical contours, the low-poly version uses triangular faces, and the simple version uses a wireframe. These are conceptual illustrations, not results from the awarded project.

Ayush Shrivastava and Rohit Narayanan selected for TCS PhD fellowships

Congratulations to Ayush Shrivastava and Rohit Narayanan on their selection for the TCS Research Scholar Program, Cycle 20. Ayush is advised by Prof. Nipun Batra and Prof. Mayank Goel; Rohit is advised by Prof. Balagopal Komarath. The fellowships are effective 1 July 2026.

Caption text
Illustration note

Ayush: a synthetic thermal patch and waveform evoke thermal health sensing. Rohit: a small monotone arithmetic formula evokes his work on bounded-depth formula complexity. These are original topic illustrations, not research measurements, paper figures or claims about the funded fellowship proposals.

Anirban Dasgupta receives STOC 2024 Test of Time Award

Prof. Anirban Dasgupta, Ravi Kumar and Tamás Sarlós received the STOC 2024 10-year Test of Time Award for “A Sparse Johnson-Lindenstrauss Transform”, published in 2010. Congratulations to the authors.

Caption text
Illustration note

A sparse matrix maps a longer coordinate vector to a shorter one, illustrating the dimension-reduction idea in the awarded paper. The displayed toy matrix illustrates shape and sparsity only; it is not the paper’s construction and does not claim a distance-preservation guarantee.
